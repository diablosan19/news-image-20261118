SUMMIT EVE

THREE LEADERS, ONE CITY: THE SHENZHEN SUMMIT

Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on Wednesday and Donald Trump flew in, ahead of Thursday's first-ever trilateral summit — Ukraine, Iran and nuclear arms control top the agenda of the three most powerful men on earth.

MIDDLE EAST

Iran strikes shipping near Hormuz and fires on a US base in Jordan; Bahrain and Kuwait intercept its drones. A tanker burns in the strait; oil nears $93.

UKRAINE

KERCH BRIDGE HIT FOR A SECOND NIGHT

Ukraine strikes the Crimea bridge again overnight — and Russia answers by hitting two bridges in Kyiv, on the eve of the Shenzhen summit.

WASHINGTON

PELTOLA FLIPS ALASKA: SENATE GOES 56–44

Democrat Mary Peltola defeats Senator Dan Sullivan — and becomes the first Alaska Native ever elected to the US Senate.

HEALTH

The Bundibugyo outbreak has killed more than 5,400 people. The WHO keeps it a global emergency — children are bearing the heaviest toll.

Antalya's first deal text demands an end to fossil fuels and $120bn a year for adaptation — with 144 clauses disputed and Friday's deadline looming.
