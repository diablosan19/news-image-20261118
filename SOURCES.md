## Health — Ebola (Bundibugyo) in DR Congo passes 11,000 cases

- **UN News, 17 Nov** (WHO): opening the third meeting of WHO's Emergency
  Committee, Director-General Tedros said the outbreak "has more than
  doubled in three months, with more than 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths
  now reported in 67 health zones across eight provinces"; children
  account for more than a quarter of confirmed cases and a third of
  deaths; 400–500 new cases a week.
- **ECDC epidemiological update**: 11,114 confirmed cases and 5,433 deaths
  as of 11 November (data to 10 Nov); later WHO snapshot via ECDC: 11,376
  cases, 5,568 deaths as of 15 Nov.
- **Zark News explainer, 18 Nov**: "On November 18 the WHO said the
  outbreak remains a public health emergency of international concern,
  following the Emergency Committee's advice."
- **Mwenendo News** (Reuters): deaths 5,568, cases 11,376; no licensed
  vaccine or specific treatment for Bundibugyo virus disease (WHO).
- Page wording uses the conservative WHO round numbers (11,000+ cases,
  5,400+ deaths) rather than any single day's tally.
